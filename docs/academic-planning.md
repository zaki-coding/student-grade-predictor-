# Academic planning

## Course grades

Each assessment has a weight and an optional percentage mark. A blank mark means the assessment is unfinished. A recorded zero is a completed mark. The calculator adds each weight times its mark divided by 100 to find the percentage points earned toward the course. The completed average uses only the weight with recorded marks.

For the average needed to reach a target, divide the points still needed by the remaining weight and multiply by 100. The remaining weight includes unfinished assessments and any weight you haven't assigned. A required average above 100% means the target is out of reach.

In an individual scenario, enter assumed marks for selected unfinished assessments. The shared assumption covers everything else that's still outstanding. To solve for a final exam, leave its mark blank and choose assumptions for the other unfinished work. For a midterm worth 30% scored at 75% and assignments worth 20% scored at 90%, the points earned are 40.5. On a final worth 50%, reaching 80% overall requires (80 − 40.5) ÷ 0.5 = 79%.

## Semester planning

The planner and calculator use the same course records. Edit the course credit hours, assessment type, due date, weight, mark, and target from the workspace. Completion by count treats every assessment equally. The grade calculator separately shows completed weight. Deadlines use the dates you entered and include only unfinished assessments with a date.

## GPA

Match your institution's percentage thresholds and grade points before using the GPA results. The semester GPA is the sum of each course's credit hours times its grade points, divided by total credit hours. The cumulative GPA combines semesters by the same method. Prior semester GPAs must use the same scale and should be entered in chronological order. Projections use any hypothetical final grades you enter and may differ from your eventual results.

The future GPA calculation spreads the points still needed for a cumulative target over the future credits. For example, with 30 completed credits at 3.0, reaching 3.2 after 15 more credits requires a 3.6 future GPA.

## Workspace files

Version 3 JSON retains the course records, targets, and percentage thresholds from version 2. It adds optional assessment fields for type and ISO date and a planning object with course credits, a GPA scale, and prior semester GPAs. Version 1 and 2 files still load. For older workspaces, courses start at 3 credit hours and GPA starts with an editable 4.0 scale. Workspace records are held in the current session. Download the JSON to keep a copy.
